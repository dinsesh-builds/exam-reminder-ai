# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Assume good intent, give specific feedback and keep discussion about the work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and deliberate disruption are not welcome in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Open a confidential report through GitHub's private reporting channel, or contact the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Consequences range from a warning to a ban.
